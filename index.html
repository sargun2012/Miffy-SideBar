About Miffy 🤍 ૮꒰ • ༝ • ꒱ა 🤍

Miffy is a little white rabbit created by Dutch artist Dick Bruna. She is known for her simple design, tiny dot eyes, cross-shaped mouth, and calm, cozy style. Her minimal appearance has made her one of the most recognizable characters in children's illustration.
The Miffy aesthetic is soft, simple, playful, and comforting. Clean shapes, gentle colors, cute decorations, cozy objects, and little everyday moments all fit naturally into the world of Miffy. That's the feeling we want our club to have too.
